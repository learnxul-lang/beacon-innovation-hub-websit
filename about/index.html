Built by innovators.Designed for impact

Beacon Innovation Hub was founded in November 2025 as a collaborative space where knowledge, curiosity and technical ability can become solutions that matter.

Our 13-member team contributes across operational, technical and research-focused roles. Mr Philani Gabriel Marapira serves as Founder & Vision Leader; Mr Samkelo Mthembu and Mr Amahle Ndumiso Nqubeko Sibisi serve as Technical Leaders; Mr Kwanele Philasande Ntshantshali and Mr Sanele Shabalala serve as Project Managers; Mr Slindokuhle Gcina Mbhamali serves as Secretary & Treasurer; Ms Aseza Mtshali serves as Secretary; and Ms Ayanda Mabaso serves as Treasurer.

These roles strengthen coordination, communication and project delivery, but they do not define control or authority. At BIH, progress is driven by shared contribution. Every member brings a different perspective, every department supports the others, and every project grows through cooperation.

Our mission is to create an environment where skills in software development, information technology, coding, data, robotics and electronics can be strengthened and applied to create meaningful industry opportunities, solve community and business challenges, and contribute to sustainable economic growth.
